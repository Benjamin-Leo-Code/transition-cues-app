# ReadySteady

Transition Cues

Familiar place, transport and bring-along pictures for a calm transition.

Choose a place and transport and optionally up to four things to bring and the people joining you. People appear as circular portraits. Crop new or existing photos in the picture library. Show the plan on the tablet in portrait or landscape. Tap five times within three seconds to finish. Manage your family's photos from your phone.

Install from the [ReadySteady custom repository](https://github.com/Benjamin-Leo-Code/transition-cues-app). Home Assistant handles installation, updates, starting and stopping. Photos and settings are kept in the add-on's private persistent storage.

See the **Documentation** tab for setup, backups and troubleshooting, and the **Changelog** tab for release changes.
